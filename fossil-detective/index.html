🔍 Welcome, Fossil Detective!

This is the Grand Staircase-Escalante in southern Utah — one of the most complete fossil records on Earth! The colorful rock layers you see were formed over hundreds of millions of years. Each layer tells a story about an ancient environment.

🎯 Your mission: Explore the rock layers, discover hidden fossils, and figure out what Utah looked like millions of years ago!
