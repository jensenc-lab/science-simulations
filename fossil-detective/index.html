📖 Vocabulary
Fossil
The preserved remains or traces of an ancient organism found in rock.
Rock Layer
A band of rock formed from sediment over time. Older layers are always at the bottom!
Ancient
Something that existed very, very long ago — thousands or millions of years old.
Organism
Any living thing — plants, animals, bacteria, and fungi are all organisms!
Environment
The surroundings where an organism lives — land, water, climate, and other living things.
Extinct
A type of organism that has completely died out and no longer exists anywhere on Earth.
Paleontologist
A scientist who studies fossils to learn about ancient life and environments.
🌟 Fun Facts
  • The Grand Staircase in southern Utah has one of the most complete fossil records on Earth — millions of years of history in the rocks!
  • The oldest rock layers are always at the BOTTOM because they were deposited first, with new layers piling on top over millions of years.
  • If you find ocean fossils on a mountain, that area was once underwater! Utah was covered by oceans multiple times in its history.
