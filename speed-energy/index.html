💡 What Do You Notice?

📖 Vocabulary
Energy
What makes things move, crash, and change. A moving object has energy!
Speed
How fast something is moving.
Cause and Effect
When one thing (the cause) makes another thing happen (the effect). Kicking a ball harder (cause) makes it go faster and farther (effect).
