📖 Vocabulary
States of Matter
The different forms matter can take — solid, liquid, or gas.
Solid
Particles locked in fixed positions; they vibrate in place.
Liquid
Particles flow freely but stay close together.
Gas
Particles spread out and fill any container.
Melting
Changing from solid → liquid by adding heat energy.
Freezing
Changing from liquid → solid by removing heat energy.
Evaporating
Changing from liquid → gas by adding heat energy.
Condensing
Changing from gas → liquid by removing heat energy.
Density
How closely packed particles are — solids are densest, gases least dense.
Phase Change
The process of matter changing from one state to another.
Heat Energy
Energy that causes particles to move faster.
💡 Key Concepts
  • Adding heat energy makes particles move faster.
  • Removing heat energy makes particles move slower.
  • Density depends on how closely packed the particles are.
  • Different substances change state at different temperatures.
