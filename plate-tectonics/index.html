Vocabulary
Plate tectonics
The theory that Earth's outer shell is divided into plates that move slowly over the mantle.
Convergent boundary
Where two plates move toward each other; produces mountains, volcanoes, or trenches.
Divergent boundary
Where two plates pull apart; magma rises to form new crust and mid-ocean ridges.
Transform boundary
Where two plates grind horizontally past each other; produces earthquakes.
Subduction
When a denser plate sinks beneath another plate into the hot mantle below.
Mid-ocean ridge
An underwater mountain chain formed where oceanic plates diverge and new seafloor is created.
Rift valley
A lowland formed as continental crust stretches and pulls apart at a divergent boundary.
Trench
A deep, narrow depression on the ocean floor formed where one plate subducts beneath another.
